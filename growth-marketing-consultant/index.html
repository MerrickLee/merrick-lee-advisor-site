What does a growth marketing consultant on retainer do?

A growth marketing consultant on retainer acts as your senior marketing advisor without a full-time hire. With my Advisory Retainer you get two strategy calls a month, a monthly KPI review, oversight of your agencies and vendors, and quick answers between calls. It's advice and oversight. Execution stays with your team, your agency or Croes Ave.

FAQ

Advisory Retainer questions

What is a marketing retainer?

A marketing retainer is a fixed monthly fee for ongoing work. Mine is advisory: strategy calls, KPI reviews and vendor oversight, not campaign execution.

How much is a marketing consultant retainer fee?

My Marketing Advisory Retainer starts at $3k a month with a three-month minimum, then runs month to month.

Marketing advisor vs. fractional CMO: what's the difference?

An advisor guides and reviews; you keep ownership. A Fractional CMO owns the plan, budget and team and is accountable for results.

Can I start without an audit?

Yes, but an audit sets a baseline. If you take the Growth Marketing Audit first, its fee is credited toward the retainer.

How fast do you reply between calls?

Within one business day.

Do you work with tree care or lawn care companies?
