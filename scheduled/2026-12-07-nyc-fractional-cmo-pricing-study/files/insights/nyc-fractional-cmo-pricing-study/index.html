Buyers looking for a fractional CMO in New York City usually run into the same problem: lots of broad "$10k to $20k a month" estimates, and very few providers stating what they actually charge, how many days a week you get, or how long you are locked in.

On November 25, 2026, we audited the public pages and directory profiles of 25 fractional CMOs and fractional CMO firms serving New York City, including my own practice (Merrick Lee Jr.). Everything below reflects only what those 25 providers publish in the open, with every figure linked to its source.

Sample and method

To reflect the options a New York City founder or executive actually sees when researching fractional CMOs in 2026, we started from the providers surfaced by major New York City editorial lists and directories and added direct NYC operator sites:

  • All 10 practitioners listed in TheCMO.com's Top 10 Fractional CMOs in New York (updated October 7, 2026).
  • Claimed or self-supplied New York City profiles on RankedCMO New York and CMO Index New York.
  • New York City operators and national firms serving New York City cited on Web Tonic's New York list, Clutch New York, Fractional Shortlist, and direct NYC search, plus merricklee.com.

Of the 25 providers, 20 list New York City as a primary base or headquarters, and 5 operate nationally or from other cities while targeting New York City via dedicated NYC pages or NYC directory placements. Every page was fetched and checked on November 25, 2026.

Method limits: This is a convenience sample of 25 public pages and profiles, not a census of every fractional CMO in the tri-state area. Custom quotes given after discovery calls are not visible here, and self-reported directory figures on RankedCMO or CMO Index may differ from what a practitioner quotes directly. My own practice is included neutrally as 1 of the 25 audited pages, listed alphabetically with everyone else.

What New York City providers publish on pricing

Out of 25 audited providers, 15 (60%) publish at least one rate or package price, while 10 (40%) publish no price at all and require a discovery call or diagnostic first. Only 10 of 25 (40%) publish prices on their own company website. The other five — Alexia Palau, Elizabeth Dimond, Matthew Simpson, Michael Fertman and Shira Stember — publish rates only on RankedCMO or CMO Index. They stay in the sample, and the source table marks those five as directory only.

Even among the 15 that publish numbers, they are not all pricing the same product. The published figures fall into four distinct structures:

1. Pure fractional CMO monthly retainers (10 providers)

Ten providers publish a monthly retainer for part-time executive marketing leadership without bundling full agency execution into the base fee:

  • $2,000/month: Saad Afsar (Firefly) states engagements start at $2,000/mo for roughly five hours a week after a free audit. Alexia Palau lists a $2,000–$15,000/mo retainer range on RankedCMO (her personal site does not publish fees).
  • $3,000 to $7,000+/month: CMOx publishes three starting tiers: CMOx Advisor from $3,000/mo, CMO Half-Day Consult from $5,000/mo, and CMO Engaged from $7,000/mo (~10 hours/week).
  • From $8,000/month: Three New York City pages anchor right at $8,000/mo: Merrick Lee Jr. (from $8,000/mo for 1–2 days/week; Advisory Retainer from $3,000/mo), Mark Gabrielli (MarkCMO) (starts at $8,000/mo on his NYC page; $2,500–$50,000/mo broad range on his main site), and Erika Velazquez Alpern (Tactile) ($8,000/mo minimum retainer).
  • From $8,500/month: Monica Hacker starts at $8,500/mo on her New York page (and lists $4,500–$15,000/mo on RankedCMO).
  • $10,000 to $25,000/month: David Berkowitz (High Caliber AI) prices Part-Time CMO at $10,000–$25,000/mo for 1–2 days/week, alongside an Advisor tier from $5,000/mo (2–4 hours/month).
  • $11,500 to $45,000/month: Michael Fertman lists $11,500–$45,000/mo on RankedCMO for enterprise B2B SaaS and fintech mandates.
  • From $20,000/month: Karran Gupta starts at $20,000/mo for companies past product-market fit, typically $15M+ in revenue.

Across those 10 published fractional CMO starting retainers, the median starting fee is $8,000/month (mean $8,500/month). That lines up closely with Go Fractional's New York benchmark (updated November 16, 2026), which estimates typical New York fractional CMO retainers at $8,400–$12,600/month based on 14 hours a week at $150–$225/hr (average $183/hr).

2. Bundled agency and outsourced CMO subscriptions (2 providers)

Two New York City-headquartered firms publish monthly pricing that bundles execution staff with marketing leadership:

  • DiscoverMyBusiness prices its all-in Outsourced CMO service at $10,000–$35,000/month, combining a named strategist with PPC, SEO, web, content and AI delivery.
  • NinjaPromo publishes monthly agency subscriptions at $4,000/mo (Starter, 40 hrs), $7,200/mo (Growth, 80 hrs) and $12,800/mo (Scale, 160 hrs), noting that a CMO is available on request only on the $12,800/mo Scale tier.

3. Hourly rates and fixed-scope entry offers

Seven providers publish hourly rates—all but one via directory profiles rather than dedicated pricing pages: Elizabeth Dimond ($200–$300/hr), Matthew Simpson ($250/hr), Shira Stember ($275/hr, while noting a shift toward value-based pricing), plus hourly ranges on RankedCMO for Monica Hacker ($125–$150/hr), Firefly ($200–$300/hr), Alexia Palau ($300–$450/hr) and Michael Fertman ($300–$600/hr).

Six providers also publish fixed-fee audits, workshops or sprints as an entry step: Merrick Lee Jr. ($1,000 Growth Audit and Strategy Workshop from $5,000), Monica Hacker ($2,500 Growth Audit), MarkCMO ($8,000+ Sprint), Tactile ($10,000+ Strategy Sprint; $15,000+ Launches), Karran Gupta ($12,500 30-day Growth Audit) and Shira Stember ($15,000 6–8 week Brand Bootcamp). Two unpriced practitioners—Firefly and theCMO.io—offer a free initial audit or go-to-market diagnosis.

4. Unpublished or non-retainer pages (10 providers)

Ten providers publish no fractional CMO fee: Large Media, theCMO.io, 25dogs, Lemniscate Growth, Christine Magda, Steve Hardy, Dialect, Thyme220, Chief Outsiders and Altanom. Notably, Dan Kerchum's personal domain redirects to Altanom, a B2B SaaS diagnostic software product (~$20/mo compared against "$5,000–$15,000+" agency/consultant costs) rather than an active fractional CMO rate card.

Engagement models: weekly cadence and minimum terms

Price means very little without knowing how much executive time you are buying and how long you are committed. Yet most public pages omit both:

  • Weekly cadence (9 of 25 stated, 36%): Providers that specify time split around 1 to 2 days a week. Merrick Lee Jr. (1–2 days/week), High Caliber AI (1–2 days/week for Part-Time CMO; 2–4 hrs/month for Advisor) and Steve Hardy's site (noting 1–2 days/week typical) sit at that benchmark. 25dogs specifies 2–3 days/week; CMOx Engaged specifies ~10 hours/week; DiscoverMyBusiness cites 10–20 hours/week equivalent; Elizabeth Dimond lists availability up to 20 hours/week; and Firefly cites about 5 hours/week (while also noting 1–2 days/week in its engagement summary). The remaining 16 providers (64%) leave weekly time unstated.
  • Minimum term or exit rules (11 of 25 stated, 44%): Seven providers set a 3- to 4-month floor on fractional CMO work: 90 days or 3 months at Merrick Lee Jr., MarkCMO (month-to-month after 90 days), Firefly, 25dogs (3–12 months), Tactile and CMOx Engaged (with a 6-month minimum on CMOx Advisor), and 4 months with a month-3 review at Monica Hacker. Lemniscate Growth advises "several quarters rather than a few weeks." Three providers highlight flexible or shorter exits: High Caliber AI (cancel with 15 days' notice and a pro-rata refund on unused time), DiscoverMyBusiness (month-to-month after onboarding) and NinjaPromo (monthly subscription with 6- and 12-month discounts). Fourteen providers (56%) publish no commitment terms.

Services vs. execution: who does the work

Almost every page lists the same strategic core: positioning, go-to-market planning, budget allocation, channel strategy, KPI reporting and managing internal staff or outside agencies. Where providers diverge is execution:

  • Leadership only (majority of solo practitioners): Operators such as High Caliber AI, Karran Gupta, 25dogs, Monica Hacker and Firefly position strictly as the executive owner—Firefly explicitly states "no agency markup"—expecting the client's team or existing vendors to run campaigns.
  • Leadership with separate optional execution: Merrick Lee Jr. (fractional CMO leadership separate from his agency Croes Ave), Dialect (fractional CMO separate from paid media, AI and software delivery) and Lemniscate Growth separate the leadership retainer from execution contracts.
  • Bundled agency delivery: DiscoverMyBusiness and NinjaPromo sell strategy and hands-on execution inside a single monthly fee. If you are weighing which model fits your team, see Fractional CMO vs. Marketing Agency.

Proof: named clients vs. unbranded claims

How providers substantiate their track record varies as widely as pricing:

  • Named client case studies with numeric metrics (6 of 25, 24%): Only six providers publish named recent or current client engagements tied to specific numbers on the audited page or site—Firefly (naming Two Boots Pizza, Boqueria, Da Andrea and Paperchase with Toast POS, Tripleseat, Meta and GA4 baselines), High Caliber AI (Storyhunter and MADTECH.AI, tagged by role), DiscoverMyBusiness (Vaziri Law Group and Hoey & Farina), Merrick Lee Jr. (four Croes Ave client results: KEB Law, Savú, Combine Academy and Clark's Diner), NinjaPromo (named case studies plus Clutch reviews) and Chief Outsiders (aggregate metrics plus named case studies).
  • Named past employers, logos or testimonials without case metrics (13 of 25, 52%): Over half the sample relies on corporate brand logos (Large Media), named past executive roles (Karran Gupta, 25dogs, Michael Fertman, Alexia Palau, Elizabeth Dimond, Shira Stember, Matthew Simpson, Steve Hardy), client lists without metrics (Tactile, Lemniscate Growth, theCMO.io) or a named quote paired with unbranded metrics (Monica Hacker).
  • Unbranded metrics or bio claims only (6 of 25, 24%): Six pages publish anonymized numbers (such as Dialect's "$2.6M to $20M" run rate without naming the company) or general background claims (MarkCMO, CMOx, Altanom, Christine Magda, Thyme220).

What this means if you are hiring in New York City

Because 40% of providers publish no fee and more than half omit weekly hours and minimum terms, a headline monthly number rarely tells you what you are comparing. Before signing with any fractional CMO in New York City, ask four questions on the first call:

  1. How many days or hours a week do we actually get? A $2,000/month engagement at five hours a week and a $15,000/month engagement at two to three days a week are different seats.
  2. What is the minimum commitment and notice period? Expect 90 days as the market norm, though month-to-month and 15-day-notice structures exist if you want lower lock-in.
  3. Is execution included or separate? Know whether the fee buys executive leadership only (so ad spend, agencies and freelancers sit on top) or an agency team with an account lead.
  4. Which results came from a fractional mandate vs. a past full-time job? Ask what changed inside a client's own analytics, CRM or point-of-sale system under a fractional engagement.

Frequently asked questions

How many New York City fractional CMOs publish pricing online?

In our November 25, 2026 audit of 25 New York City fractional CMOs and NYC-serving firms, 15 of 25 (60%) publish at least one rate or package fee publicly—10 on their own websites and 5 only on RankedCMO or CMO Index profiles. The other 10 (40%) publish no pricing.

What is the typical monthly retainer for a fractional CMO in New York City?

Among the 10 pure fractional CMO providers in our sample that publish monthly starting retainers, fees run from $2,000/month to $20,000/month with a median starting retainer of $8,000/month. That aligns with Go Fractional's November 2026 New York benchmark of $8,400–$12,600/month for 14 hours a week.

How many days a week and what minimum term do NYC fractional CMOs require?

Only 9 of 25 audited pages state weekly time publicly, centering on 1 to 2 days a week (or roughly 5 to 20 hours). Only 11 of 25 state commitment terms publicly: 7 require a 3- to 4-month (or 90-day) minimum, 1 recommends multiple quarters, and 3 offer month-to-month or 15-day cancellation terms.

Are agency execution costs included in a fractional CMO retainer?

Usually not. Most solo fractional CMOs charge strictly for leadership, strategy and vendor management, leaving ad spend and agency execution separate. Bundled outsourced CMO firms (such as DiscoverMyBusiness at $10,000–$35,000/month or NinjaPromo's $12,800/month Scale tier) include execution teams inside one monthly fee.
