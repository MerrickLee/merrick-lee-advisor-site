What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO, or fractional chief marketing officer, is a senior marketing executive who works part time, usually one or two days a week. They own the growth plan, budget, team and agencies. Some companies call the role a fractional head of growth or fractional chief growth officer. I offer it to established and PE-backed companies.

FAQ

Fractional CMO questions

What does a fractional CMO do?

A fractional CMO, or fractional chief marketing officer, sets the growth strategy, owns the marketing budget, leads the team and agencies, and reports results to leadership or the board, working part time.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

My Fractional CMO engagement starts at $8k a month for one to two days a week, with a 90-day minimum.

Fractional CMO vs. marketing agency: which do I need?

An agency executes campaigns. A fractional CMO decides what the agency should do, sets the budget and holds it accountable. Many companies need both.

Chief Growth Officer vs. CMO: what's the difference?

A CMO usually owns marketing. A Chief Growth Officer owns growth more broadly: marketing, the funnel, and how leads turn into revenue. As a fractional role, the titles often overlap.
