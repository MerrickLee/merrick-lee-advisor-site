Why Croes Ave

Strategic growth advisor

What I do is different from an agency

A strategic growth advisor works out where your growth should come from and builds the plan to get there. I look at your positioning, channels, budget, funnel and reporting together, set priorities and KPIs, then hold your team, agencies and vendors to them. You get senior marketing judgment without hiring a full-time executive.

An agency is paid to run campaigns. My job is to decide which campaigns are worth running, what they should cost and how we'll know they worked. When you need hands on keyboards, my team at Croes Ave can do the work, or I'll direct the people you already have.

FAQ

Questions I hear on fit calls

What does a strategic growth advisor do?

A strategic growth advisor decides where growth should come from and builds the plan to get there: positioning, channels, budget, funnel and KPIs. Then they hold the team and agencies accountable to it. It's senior marketing leadership focused on revenue, without a full-time executive hire.

What's the difference between a fractional CMO and a marketing agency?

An agency executes campaigns. A fractional CMO decides what the agency should do, sets the budget, owns the KPIs and reports results to you or your board. Many companies need both. As your fractional CMO, I manage the agencies on your behalf.

Do you work with companies in NYC?

Yes. I'm based in New York City and work with companies across the city, and with clients across the US remotely. If you want a marketing consultant in NYC who can also be in the room for a workshop, that's me.

Do you work with tree care or lawn care companies?

No. Because of my exclusive partnership with Almstead, I don't take on tree care, lawn care or landscaping clients. If that's your industry, Almstead is in great hands.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

My Fractional CMO engagement starts at $8k a month for one to two days a week, with a 90-day minimum. If you need less, the Advisory Retainer starts at $3k a month and a Growth Audit starts at $3.5k.
