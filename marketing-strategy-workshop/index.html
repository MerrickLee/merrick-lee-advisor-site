What does a marketing strategy consultant do in a workshop?

A marketing strategy consultant helps your leadership team decide who to target, how to position the business, which channels to fund, how to split the budget and which KPIs to track. In my Marketing Strategy Workshop we do that in a half or full day, and you get a written plan within five business days.

FAQ

Strategy Workshop questions

What happens in a marketing strategy workshop?

We review your numbers, define your ideal customer and positioning, choose the channel mix, set a budget split and agree on the KPIs leadership will review. The output is a written plan.

Half day or full day?

A half day suits one focused question. A full day covers positioning, channels, budget and KPIs end to end.

Who should attend a marketing strategy session?

The owner or CEO, whoever owns sales and marketing, and finance if budget is on the table. Usually three to six people.

Is the workshop in person or remote?

Either. In person works best in the New York City area. Travel can be arranged.

What happens after the workshop?

You get the written plan and a follow-up call. Ongoing support is available through the Marketing Advisory Retainer.
